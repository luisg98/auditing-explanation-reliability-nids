Warnecke et al. evaluate explanation accuracy, sparsity and security-oriented
criteria, including efficiency, for malware and vulnerability models
\cite{warnecke2020evaluating}. Kumar and Thing compare explanations of deep
network intrusion detectors using agreement and security-focused criteria
\cite{kumar2024evaluating}. Maseno et al. assess SHAP through permutation
importance, retraining, feature-removal and spurious-feature diagnostics on
ToN-IoT and UNSW-NB15, using logistic regression, random forests and XGBoost
\cite{maseno2026reliability}. Our contribution is incremental to these
multidimensional evaluations; different models, targets and interventions
preclude importing their numerical method rankings.

Evaluation procedures themselves require scrutiny. MetaQuantus examines the
behaviour of explanation-quality estimators~\cite{hedstrom2023metaquantus}; ROAD
and deletion-benchmark analyses expose dependence on removal rules and induced
inputs~\cite{rong2022road,wang2024deletion}. We address this dependence directly
by retaining fixed accounts and targets while changing replacement operators.
We also distinguish independently resampled LIME/KernelSHAP accounts from
IG quadrature sensitivity and deterministic repetition. This prevents several
different notions of consistency from becoming a single ranking of explainers.
